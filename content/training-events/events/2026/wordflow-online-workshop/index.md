Working with interviews, social media, news articles, or any other text corpus, but not with Python or R? LDaCA Wordflow is a free text analytics app built for exactly that, no code required: concordance, word frequency, trends, topic modelling, quotation extraction, and now AI-assisted annotation, all point-and-click, with your data visible at every step.

This online workshop ran as two 90-minute Zoom sessions. The morning session provided a guided tour of the workspace and demonstrated a research workflow built live. The afternoon session provided hands-on experience with Wordflow's new AI Annotation Tool.
