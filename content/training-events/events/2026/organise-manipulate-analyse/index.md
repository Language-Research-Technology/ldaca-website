Part of the HASS and Indigenous Research Data Commons Webinar Series.

This webinar introduces LDaCA Wordflow, a free, open-source app from the Language Data Commons of Australia that brings analysis of large text corpora to anyone, no programming required. The session covers corpus-scale analysis in a point-and-click interface: word frequencies, concordances, trends over time and topic models. You’ll also see this year’s new AI-assisted annotation in action, and the design choices that keep every result traceable to the source text. Your own codebook and hand-coded sample set the standard before a model codes the rest.

The session covers 2 ways to work: in the browser on ARDC infrastructure with nothing to install, or as a desktop app so sensitive data never leaves your machine. You will be able to judge where this could fit in your own research workflow.

**Who should attend**
Researchers in humanities, arts, social sciences and Indigenous studies.

It is of particular interest for:
- Researchers with interview transcripts, survey free text or field notes who want computational help without giving up close reading
- Historians, political scientists, linguists and media scholars working with newspapers, Hansard or social media at a scale beyond manual reading
- GLAM staff and collection custodians who want to see what researchers can do with digitised text
- Researchers and custodians with ethics approval to work with sensitive or access-restricted text, including Indigenous data, who need analysis to stay on their own machine
- Research support staff, librarians and trainers who advise researchers on text analysis tools
