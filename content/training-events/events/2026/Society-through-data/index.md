What do conversations recorded in regional Australia, government administrative data on household income and a torrent of social media posts have in common?

They're all data about how Australians live, speak and connect. Three national research infrastructure projects are building the tools to make sense of it all.

This session showed what it might look like when they work together.


***Speakers***

- Professor Michael Haugh, Project Director and Lead Chief Investigator, Language Data Commons of Australia
- Professor Nicholas Carah, Director of the Centre for Digital Cultures & Societies and Deputy Director, Australian Internet Observatory
- Professor Wojtek Tomaszewski, Director, Social Science Research Infrastructure Network
- Betsy Alpert, Research Data Scientist, Reusable and Accessible Public Interest Documents (RAPID) Project
