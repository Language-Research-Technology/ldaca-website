Do you work with text (interviews, social media, news, speeches, archives, survey comments) but not with Python or R? LDaCA Wordflow is a free text analysis app built for exactly that: word frequency, concordance, trends over time, topic modelling and, new this year, GenAI-assisted annotation, all point-and-click, with your data visible at every step.

This in-person, 3-hour workshop at the University of Melbourne, co-hosted by the Melbourne Data Analytics Platform (MDAP), the Language Data Commons of Australia (LDaCA) and the Sydney Informatics Hub, is an introduction for people who have never used it. 

**Who it’s for**
- researchers at any stage, from HDR students to senior academics, in any field where text is data. No programming experience needed. Open to researchers from all disciplines.
